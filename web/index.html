About Veena Music

Founded with a mission to preserve and promote the rich musical heritage of Rajasthan, Veena Music brings together traditional and contemporary Rajasthani artists on one platform. From timeless bhajans to modern folk renditions, our library celebrates the diversity and depth of Rajasthani music culture.
